About this page

Where the recipes come from. Every recipe here was converted from an original document (a PDF, a Word file or a spreadsheet) and then checked word for word: each word and number of the original appears in the recipe, and nothing was added. Scans and photos have no text to check against: they were transcribed by reading the image, and their footer gives the transcription's certainty (⚠ marks one below 95%: check it against the original). Recipes checked word for word say Certainty: 100%. The footer of every recipe names its original file (its path in the collection, the pages, identical copies) and a trace id, so the original can be found. A recipe edited or added by hand says so at the bottom.

Finding recipes. Search works on titles and ingredients, with or without accents. Pick a Course, Diet, Cuisine or Ingredient to narrow the list; greyed-out choices would leave nothing. Click a label on a recipe to filter by it. Press / to jump to the search box.

Cooking. Use ½× to 4× to scale the measured ingredients (baker's percentages stay as they are). Tap an ingredient or a step to tick it off. ⤢ shows only the recipe; ⎙ prints it.

Adding and editing. Use + New, or ✎ on a recipe, to add or change recipes, and 🗑 to remove one. Changes are kept in this browser until you Export: that saves a new copy of this file, with your changes, which you can open, share and edit further.

Favourites and notes are saved in this browser only, on this device. They are never uploaded, and they are lost if you clear the browser's site data or use a private window.

Labels (diet, cuisine, ingredients) are detected automatically, so please check the ingredients for allergies.
