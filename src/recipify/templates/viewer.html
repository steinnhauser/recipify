About this page

Where the recipes come from. Every recipe here was converted from an original document (a PDF or a Word file) and then checked word for word: each word and number of the original appears in the recipe, and nothing was added. Documents that could not be checked this way (scans, photos, cookbooks, spreadsheets) are not shown yet.

Finding recipes. Search works on titles and ingredients, with or without accents. Pick a Course, Diet, Cuisine or Ingredient to narrow the list; greyed-out choices would leave nothing. Click a label on a recipe to filter by it. Press / to jump to the search box.

Cooking. Use ½× to 4× to scale the measured ingredients (baker's percentages stay as they are). Tap an ingredient or a step to tick it off. ⤢ shows only the recipe; ⎙ prints it.

Favourites and notes are saved in this browser only, on this device. They are never uploaded, and they are lost if you clear the browser's site data or use a private window.

Labels (diet, cuisine, ingredients) are detected automatically, so please check the ingredients for allergies.
