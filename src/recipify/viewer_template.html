About this page

Finding recipes. Type in the search box (ingredients work too) and/or pick Course, Diet, Cuisine or Ingredient. Greyed-out choices would leave no recipes with your current selection; the number shows how many would remain. Clear all resets everything. Click a label on a recipe to filter by it.

Favourites and notes. The ★ and your notes are saved only in this browser, on this device, using its local storage. They are never uploaded and no one else can see them. They stay after closing the page, but are lost if you clear the browser's site data, use a private window, or open the file in another browser or on another device. Moving the file to a different folder may also start you with an empty list.

Good to know. Labels (diet, cuisine, ingredients) are detected automatically from the text, so please check the ingredients for allergies. Recipes marked with a warning may contain reading errors from scanned originals. Use ⎙ to print a recipe. Drag the divider between the list and the recipe to resize (double-click resets). ⤢ shows only the recipe; “Exit fullscreen” or Esc returns. Press / to jump to the search box.
