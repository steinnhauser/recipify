# Dark chocolate mousse

Serves 6. Dessert.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dark chocolate (70%)
- 4 eggs, separated
- 30 g sugar
- 250 ml whipping cream
- a pinch of salt

## Method

1. Melt the chocolate gently over a water bath and let it cool slightly.
2. Whisk the egg yolks into the chocolate one by one.
3. Whip the cream to soft peaks. In a clean bowl, whisk the egg whites with the salt, then add the sugar and whisk to glossy peaks.
4. Fold the cream into the chocolate, then the egg whites in two additions.
5. Spoon into glasses and chill for at least 3 hours.
