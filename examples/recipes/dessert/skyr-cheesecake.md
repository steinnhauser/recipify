# Skyr cheesecake with blueberries

Serves 8. No-bake dessert.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
| --- | --- |
| 200 g | digestive biscuits |
| 80 g | butter, melted |
| 500 g | vanilla skyr |
| 250 ml | whipping cream |
| 60 g | icing sugar |
| 3 | gelatine leaves |
| 250 g | blueberries |
| 2 tbsp | sugar |

## Method

1. Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 22 cm tin. Chill.
2. Soak the gelatine in cold water. Warm 3 tbsp of the cream, dissolve the squeezed gelatine in it and stir into the skyr with the icing sugar.
3. Whip the rest of the cream softly and fold through. Pour over the base and chill for at least 4 hours.
4. Simmer the blueberries with the sugar for 3 minutes, cool, and spoon over the cheesecake before serving.
