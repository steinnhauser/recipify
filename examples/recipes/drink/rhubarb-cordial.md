# Rhubarb and ginger cordial

Makes about 1 litre. Mix 1:5 with sparkling water for a summer drink.

## Ingredients

- 1 kg rhubarb, chopped
- 500 g sugar
- 500 ml water
- 40 g fresh ginger, sliced
- 1 lemon, zest and juice

## Method

1. Simmer the rhubarb, water, ginger and lemon zest for 15 minutes until the rhubarb collapses.
2. Strain through a cloth without pressing, so the juice stays clear.
3. Return to the pan with the sugar and lemon juice and simmer for 3 minutes until dissolved.
4. Bottle while hot in sterilised bottles and keep in the fridge for up to a month.
