# Plokkfiskur (Icelandic fish stew)

Serves 4. A creamy mash of white fish and potatoes, traditionally eaten with dark rye bread and butter.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
| --- | --- |
| 600 g | cod or haddock, skinless |
| 500 g | floury potatoes |
| 1 | onion, finely chopped |
| 50 g | butter |
| 3 tbsp | flour |
| 400 ml | milk |
| | salt, white pepper, chives |

## Method

1. Boil the potatoes in their skins until tender, then peel and break into rough chunks.
2. Poach the fish in lightly salted water for 6-8 minutes. Drain and flake.
3. Melt the butter and soften the onion without colour. Stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk a little at a time to make a thick white sauce.
4. Fold in the fish and potatoes, breaking them up but keeping some texture. Season well with salt and plenty of white pepper.
5. Serve hot, topped with chives, with buttered rye bread on the side.
