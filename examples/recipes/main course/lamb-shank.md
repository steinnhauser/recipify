# Slow-braised lamb shanks with root vegetables

Serves 4. Main course.

## Ingredients

- 4 lamb shanks
- 2 carrots, 1 celeriac, 1 swede, cut into large chunks
- 1 onion, 4 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 300 ml red wine
- 700 ml lamb or beef stock
- rosemary, bay leaf, salt, pepper, rapeseed oil

## Method

1. Season the lamb and brown well on all sides in oil in a heavy casserole. Remove.
2. Brown the vegetables lightly, add the tomato paste and cook for a minute.
3. Deglaze with red wine, reduce by half, then add stock, herbs and the lamb.
4. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2.5-3 hours, until the meat falls from the bone.
5. Lift out the lamb and vegetables. Reduce the sauce until glossy and pour over.
