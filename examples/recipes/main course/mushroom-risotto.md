# Wild mushroom risotto

Serves 4. Vegetarian main course.

## Ingredients

- 300 g arborio rice
- 400 g mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 15 g dried porcini, soaked in 200 ml hot water
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 150 ml dry white wine
- 1.2 l hot vegetable stock
- 60 g butter
- 60 g grated parmesan
- thyme, salt and pepper

## Method

1. Fry the fresh mushrooms in half the butter over high heat until golden. Season and set aside.
2. In the same pan, sweat the onion and garlic. Add the rice and toast for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the wine and let it evaporate. Add the chopped porcini and their strained soaking liquid.
4. Add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring, for 18-20 minutes until the rice is creamy but still has bite.
5. Off the heat, beat in the remaining butter, the parmesan and the fried mushrooms. Rest 2 minutes, then serve.
