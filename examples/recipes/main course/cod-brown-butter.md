# Cod with brown butter, dill and new potatoes

Serves 4. Main course.

## Cod

- 4 cod loins, about 160 g each
- 10 g salt
- rapeseed oil

### Method

1. Salt the cod 20 minutes before cooking, then pat dry.
2. Cook skin side down in a hot pan with oil for 4 minutes, turn and rest off the heat for 2 minutes.

## Brown butter sauce

- 120 g butter
- 1 lemon, juice
- 2 tbsp capers
- 1 bunch dill, chopped

### Method

1. Cook the butter until it smells nutty and turns golden brown.
2. Take off the heat, add lemon juice and capers, then the dill just before serving.

## New potatoes

- 600 g new potatoes
- 20 g butter
- salt

### Method

1. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15-18 minutes, drain and toss with the butter.
