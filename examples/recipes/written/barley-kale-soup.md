# Barley and kale soup

Serves 4. Written straight in markdown, so the file is the recipe.

## Ingredients

- 150 g pearl barley
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1.5 l vegetable stock
- 200 g kale, stalks removed
- 1 tbsp butter
- salt and pepper

## Method

1. Melt the butter and soften the onion and carrots for 5 minutes.
2. Add the barley and stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 35 minutes.
3. Tear in the kale and simmer for 5 more minutes. Season to taste.

> Thickens overnight: loosen with a little water when reheating.
