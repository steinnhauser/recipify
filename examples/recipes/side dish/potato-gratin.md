# Potato gratin

Serves 6 as a side dish.

## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg waxy potatoes, thinly sliced
- 400 ml whipping cream
- 200 ml milk
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 100 g grated cheese
- nutmeg, salt, pepper, butter for the dish

## Method

1. Warm the cream, milk and garlic with salt, pepper and a little nutmeg.
2. Layer the potatoes in a buttered dish, pouring the cream mixture over as you go.
3. Cover with foil and bake at 180°C for 45 minutes.
4. Uncover, scatter with cheese and bake another 25 minutes until golden and tender.
