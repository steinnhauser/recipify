# Pönnukökur (Icelandic thin pancakes)

Makes about 15. Breakfast or afternoon coffee.

## Ingredients

- 250 g flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 500-600 ml milk
- 50 g butter, melted
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Method

1. Whisk the dry ingredients together. Add the eggs and half the milk and whisk to a smooth batter.
2. Whisk in the rest of the milk, the melted butter and the vanilla. The batter should be thin, like single cream. Rest for 15 minutes.
3. Cook very thin pancakes in a hot pan, about 30 seconds per side.
4. Serve rolled with sugar, or folded with jam and whipped cream.
