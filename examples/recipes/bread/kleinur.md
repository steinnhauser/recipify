# Kleinur (Icelandic twisted doughnuts)

Makes about 40. A classic coffee-table pastry.

## Ingredients

- 500 g flour
- 150 g sugar
- 3 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tsp ground cardamom
- 50 g butter, soft
- 1 egg
- 250 ml buttermilk (or súrmjólk)
- oil or lard for deep frying

## Method

1. Mix the dry ingredients, rub in the butter, then add the egg and buttermilk to make a soft dough. Knead briefly.
2. Roll out to about 4 mm and cut into diamonds about 8 cm long. Cut a slit in the middle of each and pull one end through to make the twist.
3. Fry at 175°C, a few at a time, for 1-2 minutes per side until deep golden.
4. Drain on paper and let cool before serving.
