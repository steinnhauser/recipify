# Béarnaise sauce

Makes about 300 ml. A basic for steak and fish.

## Ingredients

- 3 egg yolks
- 250 g butter, clarified and kept warm
- 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 6 peppercorns, crushed
- fresh tarragon and chervil
- salt, lemon juice

## Method

1. Reduce the vinegar with the shallot, peppercorns and tarragon stalks to about 1 tbsp. Strain and cool.
2. Whisk the reduction with the yolks over a gentle water bath until thick and pale.
3. Off the heat, whisk in the warm butter in a thin stream to form a thick sauce.
4. Season with salt and lemon juice and stir in chopped tarragon and chervil. Keep warm, not hot.
