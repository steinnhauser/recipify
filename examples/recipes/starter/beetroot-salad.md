# Roasted beetroot with whipped skyr and hazelnuts

Serves 4 as a starter.

## Ingredients

- 4 medium beetroots
- 200 g skyr (or thick Greek yogurt)
- 1 lemon, zest and juice
- 40 g hazelnuts, toasted and crushed
- 2 tbsp honey
- rapeseed oil, flaky salt, dill

## Method

1. Wrap the beetroots in foil and roast at 200°C for about an hour, until a knife slides in easily. Peel and cut into wedges.
2. Whisk the skyr with lemon zest, a little juice and a pinch of salt until light.
3. Toss the beetroot with oil, honey and the rest of the lemon juice.
4. Spread the skyr on plates, arrange the beetroot on top and finish with hazelnuts, dill and flaky salt.
