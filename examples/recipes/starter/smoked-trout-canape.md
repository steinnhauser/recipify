# Smoked trout on rye with horseradish cream

Makes about 20 starter bites.

## Ingredients

- 6 slices dense rye bread
- 200 g hot-smoked trout or arctic char
- 100 ml whipping cream
- 2 tbsp grated horseradish
- 1 small cucumber
- pickled red onion, dill, lemon

## Method

1. Cut the rye into small squares and toast lightly.
2. Whip the cream to soft peaks and fold in the horseradish, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt.
3. Slice the cucumber into thin ribbons.
4. Top each square with a spoon of horseradish cream, a flake of trout, a cucumber ribbon, pickled onion and dill.
